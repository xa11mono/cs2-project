CS2 Manager v51 — Major Pick'Em navigation and medal layout

- Pick'Em is removed from the “Ещё” overflow menu and is now a separate top-level navigation button.
- The button is hidden before the Major Pick'Em roster lock, appears on Pick'Em Day, and remains available throughout the Major until completion.
- Removed the old Pick'Em trophy text from the season/time header and the redundant Pick'Em button in tournament management.
- Reworked the Pick'Em page into a two-column layout: the stage predictions remain on the left, and the current Major coin/medal is displayed on the right.
- The medal uses the existing generated Major-specific coin artwork and shows this Major's points, current tier, and progress to the next tier.
- Added a responsive stacked layout for narrower screens.
- Added v51 regression tests and updated the browser verification expectations.
