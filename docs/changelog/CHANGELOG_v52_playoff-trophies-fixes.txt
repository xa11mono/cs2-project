CS2 Manager v52 — playoff logos and trophy retention

1) Playoff logos
- Eliminated team crests remain in full colour in playoff brackets, including semifinal and final views.
- Loser styling remains on score/name text; the crest no longer inherits grayscale, opacity or blend-mode effects.

2) Persistent trophy history
- Added a durable team-title ledger to the awards store, separate from the bounded tournament-snapshot archive.
- Existing titles are migrated at startup from team history, archived event snapshots and player title records.
- Duplicate records are merged by team + event + season, including records that previously had different history/archive IDs.
- Old titles remain visible in team profiles, player career trophies, the trophy cabinet, Major winners, champions by year, team trophy totals and season archive after detailed bracket snapshots are pruned.
- A trophy without a retained bracket remains visible as a static record and does not open a missing event.
- Removed display caps that hid older trophy records.

3) Correct trophy tiers
- Corrected stale Tier-3 labels when the underlying event metadata identifies Tier-1.
- Tier-1, Tier-2 and Tier-3 are counted independently, alongside Major, S-Tier and Academy titles.
- Qualifiers are excluded from championship trophy records.

Validation
- Node regression tests v43 through v52 passed.
- Changed JavaScript files passed `node --check`.
