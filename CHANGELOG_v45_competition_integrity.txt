CS2 Manager v45 — Competition Integrity

Tournament identity and awards
- Upper-bracket final, lower-bracket final, and true Grand Final now have separate labels/news copy. Only the actual championship final awards the title.
- Tournament completion is committed immediately when the championship final has a winner; the bronze match no longer blocks champion assignment.
- MVP assignment is independent from team trophy insertion, so a pre-existing cup no longer suppresses MVP calculation.
- Player profiles show achievements even when no match-stat record exists. Trophy shelf defaults to All and resets when opening a different player/team profile.
- Legacy Tier-3 trophies use saved fieldProfile metadata for correct classification.

Tournament planning and Major
- Qualification and Major invite previews are prepared before tournament day and the Major roster is frozen in advance.
- Upcoming event pages show known invitees before the event starts; team directory contains qualification placements/results.
- Major calendar is split into Pick'Em Day, Stage I, Stage II, Stage III, and Champions Stage.
- Pre-start Stage I Pick'Em can be saved and carried into the live Major.
- Swiss 3:0 and 0:3 qualification buckets render team logos.
- Major Path no longer forces top-32 teams with zero invite probability into the potential invite list.

Rating and match balance
- VRS approximation now stays anchored to saved team baseline instead of globally renormalizing all teams. Combined evidence and head-to-head adjustment is bounded to ±30 points; single-match K is reduced.
- Map economy snowball is softened to reduce excessive blowouts while preserving rare upsets and dominant scorelines.

Compatibility
- Changes are additive and retain existing save data; the new qualification preview fields are persisted in season state.
