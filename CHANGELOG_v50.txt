CS2 Manager v50 — Pick'em, logos, and academy transfer fixes

Pick'em Major
- Added explicit category buttons for each prediction category. A player can select 0:3 first without completing 3:0.
- Empty prediction slots also activate their category when clicked.
- Added a short explanation of the Pick'em format: 2 picks for 3:0, 6 picks for 3:1/3:2 advancement, and 2 picks for 0:3.
- Clarified that 1:3 and 2:3 are elimination results displayed on the Swiss board, not separate official Pick'em prediction slots.

Stage II / Stage III team logos
- Logo rendering now tries all available sources in sequence: personal media library, saved team logo/photo/crest fields, and EMDB metadata.
- If one image URL is broken, the next available source is attempted before falling back to initials.

Academy recruitment / transfers
- Current professional-roster players are tagged with persistent professional-origin metadata.
- Academy signing and transfer gates now reject professional-origin players even if they have already been moved between academies or the transfer is marked forced.
- Old saves continue to migrate accidentally placed professional-origin players out of academy rosters.

Validation
- Added tests/v50-pickem-academy-logos.test.js.
- The v43–v50 JavaScript regression tests pass.
- The Playwright browser suite requires the `playwright` package, which is not installed in this environment; it was not run.
